# Sample Q&A Log

## Factual Queries

### Q1: What is the mandatory lock-in period for SBI Long Term Equity Fund?
> **Answer:** SBI Long Term Equity Fund is an Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) and comes with a mandatory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of allotment. During this period, units cannot be redeemed or switched.
> 
> **Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/en-us/equity-schemes/sbi-long-term-equity-fund
> 
> *Last updated from sources: October 2026*

---

### Q2: What is the minimum SIP amount for SBI Flexicap Fund?
> **Answer:** The minimum Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) amount for SBI Flexicap Fund is ₹500 per month.
> 
> **Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/en-us/equity-schemes/sbi-flexicap-fund
> 
> *Last updated from sources: October 2026*

---

### Q3: What is the exit load for SBI Large & Midcap Fund?
> **Answer:** For redemptions within 1 year from the date of allotment, an exit load of 1% is applicable. No exit load is charged for redemptions made after 1 year.
> 
> **Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/sid-kim/kim-sbi-large-midcap-fund.pdf
> 
> *Last updated from sources: October 2026*

---

### Q4: How can I download my Capital Gains statement?
> **Answer:** You can download your Capital Gains statement directly from the SBI Mutual Fund online portal or via the CAMS CAS download page by verifying your registered email and PAN.
> 
> **Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/en-us/investor-corner/download-account-statement
> 
> *Last updated from sources: October 2026*
